Run your event's **call for papers**, review submissions, build the agenda, and onboard
speakers — all in one open-source app you can self-host on Cloudflare.

## Features {#features}

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title='CFP Forms' icon='lucide:file-text'>
    Publish submission forms with custom fields and conditional logic. Speakers sign in
    with Google and submit talks in minutes.
  </Card>
  <Card title='Abstract Review' icon='lucide:list-checks'>
    Vote, rate, and comment on every submission. Move talks through accept and decline
    queues, then notify speakers in one click.
  </Card>
  <Card title='Agenda Builder' icon='lucide:calendar'>
    Place accepted sessions into rooms and time slots. Conflicts are detected live,
    calendar invites go out automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title='Speaker Portal' icon='lucide:users'>
    Speakers confirm participation, complete onboarding tasks, upload slides and
    headshots, and track their submissions.
  </Card>
  <Card title='Reminder Emails' icon='lucide:mail'>
    Automatic reminders for unfinished drafts and due tasks. Decision emails and ICS
    calendar invites, no configuration needed.
  </Card>
  <Card title='Embeds & Feeds' icon='lucide:code'>
    Embeddable agenda and speaker pages plus JSON and ICS feeds for your event website.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it works

<Steps>

### Create your event

Sign in with Google, create an event, and set up tracks, formats, and rooms.

### Publish your CFP

Open a submission form and share the public link. Submissions land in your abstracts
table with live status tabs.

### Review and accept

Your whole team votes on submissions. Accepted speakers automatically get portal tasks
and decision emails.

### Build the agenda

Drag accepted talks into the schedule. Publish embeds and feeds to your event website.

</Steps>
